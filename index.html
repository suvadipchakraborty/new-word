About Tharoor Words

Shashi Tharoor, the author and politician, is famous for sprinkling rare words such as floccinaucinihilipilification, farrago and rodomontade into his writing and speeches. This app helps you build a vocabulary with the same flair. Each card gives the word, how to say it, and a definition with examples.

The Tharoorian filter collects the showpiece words in that spirit. The rest are classic GRE and SAT words.

How to study

Say the word aloud, guess its meaning, then tap the card to check. Swipe left or right to move on. Save tricky words, and mark a word as mastered once you know it. Use the Saved and Mastered filters to review. Good for GRE, SAT and everyday vocabulary.

Add your own words

Fork the repository, edit words.csv (columns: word,difficulty,category), and deploy to Cloudflare Pages. Definitions load automatically.
